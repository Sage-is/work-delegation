work-delegation

Expensive models should judge. Cheap models should type.

The problem

Your premium AI assistant spends most of its tokens typing file content. Docs pages, style passes, docstring sweeps. Work a free model handles fine.

The split

work-delegation gives your assistant a wrapper called oc-edit. The assistant writes a precise brief. A free model, driven through opencode's CLI, does the typing. The assistant reads the git diff before anything counts as done. Judgment stays expensive. Typing gets cheap.

Measured, not promised

In an 8-run matrix across four task types, the free tier scored 4 for 4 with zero retries at 7 to 15 seconds per task. The paid tier matched it on quality and took 5 to 7 times longer. Every diff was reviewed line by line. The raw patches ship in the repo.

Guardrails

The delegated session cannot run git, delete recursively, fetch the web, or spawn nested delegation. A hardlink guard protects linked files. One environment variable, OC_DELEGATE=0, shuts the whole thing off.

Every harness

One make install-all covers Claude Code, Codex, and opencode itself. The same brief-delegate-review loop, whichever assistant you run.
